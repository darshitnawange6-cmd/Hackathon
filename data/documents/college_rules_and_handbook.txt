# NEXUS INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY – OFFICIAL STUDENT HANDBOOK & REGULATIONS (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

## SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE & ACADEMIC STANDING
1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement: All undergraduate students must maintain a minimum of 75% aggregate attendance across each enrolled lecture and laboratory course.
1.2 Medical Condonation: Students with documented medical leaves or approved college representations (sports/hackathons) may be granted attendance condonation down to 65% upon submitting an authorized medical certificate to the Dean of Student Affairs within 5 working days of return.
1.3 Debarment Policy: Students whose attendance falls below 65% are strictly debarred from sitting for the semester end-term examinations in that course and must repeat the course during summer term.

## SECTION 2: EXAMINATION HALL PROTOCOLS
2.1 Mandatory Hall Ticket: Entry to any mid-term or end-term examination hall requires physical presentation of the Student ID Card and printed Examination Admit Card with invigilator verification.
2.2 Permitted Equipment: Only blue/black pens, non-programmable scientific calculators (models Casio fx-82, fx-991EX or equivalent), and transparent water bottles are allowed.
2.3 Prohibited Devices: Mobile phones, smartwatches, wireless earbuds, and unauthorized study notes are strictly forbidden in exam halls. Violation results in automatic cancellation of the paper and disciplinary referral.
2.4 Late Entry: No student will be allowed inside the examination hall 15 minutes after the scheduled commencement time.

## SECTION 3: GRADING SCALE & EVALUATION
- A+ (90-100%): 10.0 Grade Points – Outstanding
- A  (80-89%):  9.0 Grade Points – Excellent
- B+ (70-79%):  8.0 Grade Points – Very Good
- B  (60-69%):  7.0 Grade Points – Good
- C  (50-59%):  6.0 Grade Points – Satisfactory
- D  (40-49%):  4.0 Grade Points – Pass
- F  (<40%):    0.0 Grade Points – Fail

## SECTION 4: LIBRARY & DIGITAL COMMONS REGULATIONS
4.1 Borrowing Quota: Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for an active period of 14 calendar days.
4.2 Renewals: Online renewals are permitted up to twice, provided no other student has placed a hold on the title.
4.3 Overdue Fines: A late return fine of $0.50 per day per book applies after the 14-day loan duration.
4.4 Quiet Study Zone: The 3rd floor of the Central Library is designated as a Strict Silence Sanctuary. Group discussions are restricted to 2nd floor pods and 1st floor open spaces.

## SECTION 5: CAMPUS HOSTEL & RESIDENCE RULES
5.1 Campus Curfew: Hostel main gates close at 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday, and 11:30 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. Late entry requires prior digital gate-pass approval from the Chief Warden.
5.2 Noise Policy: Quiet hours in student residential blocks operate between 11:00 PM and 06:00 AM daily.
